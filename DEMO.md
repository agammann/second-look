# Second Look: PayPal AI Hackathon demo runbook

A [65-second working demo](artifacts/demo/second-look-demo.webm) is recorded and visually checked for the [PayPal AI Hackathon](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/). Visible captions sit below the original app screen; [VTT captions](artifacts/demo/second-look-demo.vtt) are included separately. The video shows actual live Qwen review, saved acknowledgement, sandbox order creation, explicit capture and receipt reload. It combines four real takes. PayPal's account/approval screen is excluded, and its separately completed fictitious-buyer approval is explained in the captions. [Recording evidence](artifacts/demo/demo-evidence.json) preserves the cuts and a trimmed final reload assertion from the capture take; the last take independently verifies reload persistence. No credential entry or mock UI is in the video. The [public YouTube demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znVLaGqigrw) is published. The submission deadline is November 12, 2026, at 12:00 noon PST.

Open the loopback shop using [README.md](README.md). Judges on a new machine can install Node 24.2+, obtain Ollama and the exact local model, and configure their own free sandbox app/buyer using that guide. Explain that the six collectibles and seller listings are synthetic, and the illustrations are decorative. Second Look helps a buyer read seller wording; it does not authenticate an object, verify statements or decide whether to buy. Keep the full saved listing and the buyer's independent assessment visible.

## Recording preparation

Use only authorized PayPal sandbox accounts and test funds. Keep `.env`, API credentials, buyer passwords, Developer Dashboard account details and unrelated tabs out of the recording. Sign into sandbox privately before recording, or pause before credential entry and resume after sign-in. At checkout, use the generated Sandbox Personal account; the regular PayPal login is only for Developer Dashboard access. Do not enter a real card or bank account.

If showing an already completed receipt, identify it as the actual recorded test result; do not imply that replaying a callback created another payment. A new checkout requires its own acknowledged review and sandbox approval. Keep recorded UI actions and previously verified evidence distinct.

## Optional expanded walkthrough (target 2:40)

1. **0:00–0:20 — Purpose and full context.** Choose **Orbit Courier** and enter `Original edition`, `Complete contents` and `Original box and manual`. Read the full saved seller listing, including condition details and the seller's unverified-origin statement. Explain why a favorable excerpt needs its surrounding qualifications.
2. **0:20–0:55 — Live local suggestions.** Select **Find seller quotes**. Qwen selects source-span and neutral-question IDs from the actual listing and requirements; results may vary. An offered sentence such as “The original box, printed manual and winding key are included.” is seller wording, not proof. Compare suggestions with the full listing and identify any missing context or component ambiguity.
3. **0:55–1:15 — Independent buyer decision.** Read any neutral questions. Empty/invalid suggestions do not prove that information is absent. Make an independent assessment, acknowledge the review and select **Save my review decision**. No message is sent to a merchant, and the suggestion itself cannot acknowledge or purchase anything.
4. **1:15–2:05 — Separate PayPal approval and capture.** Create the sandbox order for the reviewed server-owned amount/version. Show the sandbox approval page and buyer's separate approval without credentials in frame. Return to the app, then explicitly request server capture. Explain that the return URL alone cannot mark the order paid.
5. **2:05–2:25 — Durable receipt.** Show the saved description, requirements, amount/version and separate provider/capture statuses. Only a verified completed capture is paid. Reload or check status to show the result persists.
6. **2:25–2:40 — Limits and access.** State that quotation integrity does not establish relevant or complete retrieval; full-text review remains required. Point judges to the public source and complete local setup instructions once those materials are reviewed and published.

## Supporting evidence outside the short video

The actual sandbox continuation completed a $48.00 USD/version-1 capture and preserved it through status checks and reload. A separate cancellation remained uncaptured. An isolated provider negative-testing simulation returned `INSTRUMENT_DECLINED` on capture (HTTP 422); local state remained ambiguous beside provider `APPROVED`, with no completed capture, and subsequent status/reload activity issued GETs without repeating the single capture POST. Identify this as controlled negative testing, rather than an ordinary buyer decline. Keep these results distinct from labeled transport mocks.

The prior actual browser QA also demonstrated a merchant edit: an old review became stale, its saved snapshot remained readable, and a fresh version required new acknowledgement. For an additional edit walkthrough, use separate demo state; preserve the completed sandbox receipt and require fresh checkout approval after a change.

The recorded suite passes 154/154 (including historical checks), prior actual browser flow 24/24 and MOCK rendering 43/43. Eight new sibling-authored v4 cases ran once after freeze: 11/12 rows were mechanically valid, with 24 exact displayed excerpts. Context omissions, excess excerpts, generic questions and one rejected empty/question combination remain limitations.
